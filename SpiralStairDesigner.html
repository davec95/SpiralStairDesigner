Headroom = underside Z of the soffit over this step, minus walking Z. Helix pie soffits are sampled at the nose i · θ (front of the tread), not the heel (i−1) · θ. Shared nosing lines (0° area) are not overhead. PLATFORM in the Overhead column is going-overlap with the 90° square — not a single radial. A pie whose going sits under the square is PLATFORM even if its nose has already cleared the far edge, or if only a sliver is under. Full vs (partial) is both-ends-in vs sliver. Default 24.6° / 18 rises: #3 partial, #4–#6 full, #7 partial. Pie numbers sit at mid-going, matching that overlap.

Pie tread k occupies the going behind its nose: plan sector ((k−1)·θ, k·θ]. The soffit is k · rise − thickness.

36×36 landing occupies 90° in the climb direction from the last pie nose (same as the square with the pole at a corner). That is one full tread past the last going, then 90° − θ more. Its soffit is floor-to-floor − thickness. If both a pie and the landing cover the step, the lower soffit wins.

Orientation offset rotates the whole plan 0–359° (1° steps). 0° = step 1 heel at the top. Positive offset is in the climb direction. CW/CCW is ascent as viewed from above.

Step #0 = ground at 0°. Steps #1…N−1 are pie treads. Rises N is floor-to-floor count including the last rise onto the platform; uniform rise = floor-to-floor / N, constrained to 7.00–9.5″. Height of pie i = i × rise. Platform height = N × rise = floor-to-floor. Open well = nothing in plan above that nose.

IRC R311.7.2 minimum is 78" (6'8"), measured vertically from the nosing.

Walkline is 12" out from the narrow end of the tread (pole radius + pole-to-tread gap + 12"), not from pole center. Nosing-to-nosing depth = r_wl · θ_rad = (2π r_wl) / treads-per-turn. Plate depth = going + 1.25" (½" past nose + ¾" past heel — IRC legal max overhang). Color on the plan spiral: red if going < 6.00" or plate < 6.75"; yellow if plate is 6.75–7.49"; green if plate ≥ 7.5". Three walkline checks: radius ≤ 24½" (center of pole to walkline), tread depth (plate) ≥ 6¾", going at walkline ≥ 6".

Going is the arc length along the walkline (12" from the narrow end of the tread) between the nosings of two adjacent treads — i.e. r_wl · θ (radians). At θ = 24.5°, 4" pole, 0.75" gap this is ~6.31". It's the separation between consecutive nosings measured at the walking path.

Plate is the usable tread arc length at the walkline: going + 1.25" (½" past the nose, ¾" past the heel). This is what you actually step on — the tread extends slightly beyond each nosing so the tread material doesn't end right at the nosing line. The color badge shows whether the plate is ≥ 7.5" (green / plenty), 6.75–7.49" (yellow / IRC spiral walkline minimum is 6¾"), or failing (red).
